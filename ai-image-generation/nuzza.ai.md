# Nuzza AI

Nuzza AI is a workspace for creating, editing, and enhancing AI images and videos with generative models. It brings several visual-production steps into one browser-based environment.

It may be useful for creators who want to generate a first concept, revise it, improve the result, and continue into motion without moving between unrelated services.
