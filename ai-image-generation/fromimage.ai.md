# FromImage AI

FromImage AI is an image-to-image generator that transforms reference photos with natural-language prompts and a selection of generative models. It gives users a starting image while allowing the style or visual treatment to be changed through text.

It may be useful for style exploration, reference-guided variations, campaign concepts, character studies, and creative edits that should retain elements of the source composition.
