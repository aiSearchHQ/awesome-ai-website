# FloorAI

FloorAI generates and edits floor plans from sketches, images, PDFs, or written instructions. It converts several common forms of early design input into a more structured spatial plan.

It may be useful for early architectural studies, property layouts, renovation ideas, converting scanned plans, and communicating a rough spatial concept.
