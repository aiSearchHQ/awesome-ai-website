# Crisp3D AI

Crisp3D AI turns text descriptions and images into 3D models intended for printing. It bridges a two-dimensional idea or written concept with a model that can move into a physical fabrication workflow.

It may be useful for prototypes, figurines, product concepts, educational models, and makers exploring an idea before detailed manual 3D modeling.
