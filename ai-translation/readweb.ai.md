# ReadWeb.ai

ReadWeb.ai provides instant web-page translation with a bilingual reading view. Showing the source and translated text together can help users follow the original context while browsing content in another language.

It may be useful for research, international news, documentation, language study, and reading websites that do not publish an official translation.
