# DeckFlow

DeckFlow is an AI translator for PowerPoint and PDF files that aims to preserve the original formatting. Its specialization in presentation and document layouts reduces the need to rebuild every translated slide manually.

It may be useful for sales decks, training materials, reports, conference presentations, and international versions of client-facing documents.
