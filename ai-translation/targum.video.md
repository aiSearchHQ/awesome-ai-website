# Targum Video

Targum Video is an AI video translation tool with downloadable translated video and subtitle outputs. It focuses on adapting an existing video rather than requiring users to rebuild localized versions from scratch.

It may be useful for creator videos, interviews, tutorials, social posts, and teams that need translated speech together with subtitles.
