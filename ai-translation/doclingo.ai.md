# Doclingo

Doclingo translates PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and image files while working to preserve the original layout. It is intended for document workflows where formatting matters alongside the translated text.

It may be useful for reports, presentations, business documents, spreadsheets, and scanned material that must remain recognizable after translation.
